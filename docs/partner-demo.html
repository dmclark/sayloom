After one sentence

Three useful questions

No survey, scoring exercise, or technical setup. The conversation is the feedback.

  1. Did the suggestion preserve what you meant?
  2. Was it clear what Sayloom changed and could remember?
  3. Would approved corrections reduce repeat effort in your normal writing?
