## Approved bounded inspection-first contract

### Scope and precedence

The approved contribution combines explicit Original-first review/save,
contextual cross-session reuse and complete management/privacy boundaries for
replacements and anchored additions/removals. It does not require broader
automatic Smart completion or a separate effectiveness assessment. Storage,
retrieval or extra highlights alone do not complete the contribution.

This section governs the selected contribution where older generated-result
state machines, replacement-only schema/API/events or Smart-only assessment
references below differ. Existing Prepared-confirmation behavior remains
intact. No historical issue graph, build prompt or assessment is activated.

### Original-bound review and independent save

Separate review starts from a nonempty selection in current Original. Freeze
exact Original, draft revision, selection offsets and selected Observed wording.
It works without a finding, successful processing result or Prepared, including
with preparation Off. The owner supplies Intended wording or explicitly adopts
and may edit an offered alternative. A finding alone supplies no Intended.

Show source, individual operation, reusable before/intended pair, applicability
restriction and all optional durable sentence text. Selections start unchecked;
one Save selected approves 1–20 selected mappings atomically. Bound each reusable
phrase side to 200 Unicode code points, applicability text to 500 and each
optional containing sentence to 500. Sentence previews are independently
editable/removable; never silently truncate or retain surrounding documents.

An addition/removal needs a reviewed, nonempty before/intended phrase pair with
enough unchanged wording to anchor the change. Review may expand the selection
within frozen Original, visibly showing the expanded Observed pair. Store the
individual operation separately from its reusable anchored pair. No empty
trigger, context-free insertion or delete-everywhere rule is saveable for reuse.

Saving does not apply wording, regenerate, confirm Prepared, change Outgoing,
copy or deliver. Inspection, editing, alternative application, dismissal and
inaction save no Personal Correction. Existing Prepared and retained Smart
review paths identify their own before/after sources and share this explicit
approval boundary. Applying wording first changes Original and requires a newly
bound review, not relocation of an obsolete one.

### Identity, authorization and transaction semantics

Equivalent mappings share owner, operation, normalized reusable before/intended
pair and the same approved restriction. Intended term alone is not identity.
Differing restriction text remains separate; do not infer semantic equivalence,
merge meanings, overwrite another mapping or broaden authorization silently.
Preserve exact approved display text, operation, provenance, revisions and
episode/history links for every repair type.

First approved save creates an enabled profile only if absent. Respect existing
global disablement. Saving an equivalent disabled mapping does not silently
re-enable it; show its state and require explicit re-enable. Adapt or retire
direct-create paths that bypass reviewed approval.

Original-first review has manual provenance and no processing prompt. Bind it
to frozen Original/selection and review identity, not Prepared. Settings alone
do not invalidate owner-supplied wording; revalidate an offered alternative
before adoption. Original edits, Clear/new passage, replacement even with
identical text, another review, reload/restart or a conflicting management
revision invalidate review. Never revive validity by text equality or searching
for another occurrence. Stale responses cannot reopen review or alter output.

Save is one transaction with an approval ID and immutable selected payload.
Same-ID/same-payload retries return the committed outcome; changed payloads
conflict. Validation/transaction failure leaves no partial batch. Management
writes are owner-scoped and revision-checked; conflicts preserve newer state.

An edit after an explicitly authorized Save is sent cannot reliably reverse a
server commit. Retire stale browser effects without claiming rollback. Make
the exact approval outcome reconcilable in management; do not resave altered
content under the same approval ID. Reload loses unsaved review, not a committed
mapping. Save failure never triggers provider retries.

Server-owned retrieval supplies only matched, enabled, approved mappings to
the existing Smart request's untrusted evidence frame. Add no model pass,

does not require Smart availability.

Conservative/Cleanup receives no Personal Corrections in its prompt, request,

Supply only reusable before/intended pairs, operations and approved restrictions.
Historical sentences, occurrence history/counts, unrelated mappings, local IDs

Track mapping/profile revisions in request-local state. Edit initiation,

and actionable suggestions; later processing uses current authorized state.
Do not guess how to subtract a mapping's influence from a mixed result.
Management itself never regenerates or rewrites current Prepared.

### Outcome and scope

The owner can select current Original wording, supply Intended wording, inspect
individual changes and explicitly save selected Personal Corrections. This works
with preparation Off, without Prepared, without a finding and without a provider
request.

Saving does not apply wording, regenerate, confirm Prepared, change Outgoing,
copy or deliver. It authorizes approved mappings for later contextual reuse,
but this increment does not implement that reuse.

Included:

- Original-selected manual review, with replacements and anchored additions/removals.
- Atomic approval of 1–20 selected mappings.
- Restriction-sensitive identity and independent mapping state.
- Immutable approval outcomes, retries and reconciliation.
- Read-only inspection of saved fields and approval details.
- Preservation of existing Prepared confirmation and separate saving review.

Excluded:

- Retrieval, prompting, personalized findings or automatic correction.
- Full management editing, deletion, export or registered-state purge.
- Activity-history recording, prior-passage reuse or inheritance.
- Provider changes, efficacy studies and ordinary-use activation.

The read-only management surface serves this isolated engineering increment,
not the complete approved contribution.

### Individual changes and reusable phrases

Use these names consistently in the UI, API and domain records:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `operation` | `replacement`, `addition` or `removal`. |
| `reusable_before_text` | Nonempty approved phrase that becomes the future trigger. |
| `reusable_intended_text` | Nonempty approved intended phrase, retaining any unchanged anchor. |
| `before_change_span` | Individual changed range within the reusable before phrase. |
| `after_change_span` | Corresponding range within the reusable intended phrase. |

Changed wording is obtained from the spans; no second stored text copy is needed.

| Operation | Individual before change | Individual after change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replacement | Nonempty | Nonempty |
| Addition | Empty | Nonempty |
| Removal | Nonempty | Empty |

A removal has empty individual after wording, not an empty reusable intended
phrase. The latter retains unchanged anchor wording. All spans are half-open
Unicode code-point ranges and must slice their corresponding phrase exactly.
The server independently validates the operation; submitted spans do not
establish its classification.

### Opening and editing Original review

A desktop-accessible **Review for Personal Corrections** action requires a
nonempty selection in current Original. Before focus moves, capture in browser
memory passage identity, draft revision, exact frozen Original, initial selection
offsets and selected wording, and a new review identity.

The review labels its source **Selected Original**. Intended begins empty; the
owner supplies it. An explicitly adopted, already available alternative may
populate it only after its occurrence and revision are revalidated. Adoption
makes no provider request.

Empty Intended is valid input for proposing a removal. It becomes saveable only
after a nonempty reusable anchored pair is established.

Derive ordered candidates using the existing lexical-operation vocabulary. Each
candidate shows the individual operation and changed wording, reusable before
and intended phrases, any expansion beyond the initial selection, and a
selection checkbox, initially unchecked.

Use one applicability restriction for the selected batch, matching the existing
approval surface. Separate approvals can save different restrictions.
Per-candidate restriction editing is deferred. Optional Observed and Intended
sentences are episode-level fields; each can be independently edited or removed.

Editing a candidate unchecks that candidate. Changing the shared restriction or
sentence fields unchecks all candidates. Saving requires explicit selection
after those changes. More than 20 candidates may remain visible, but only 1–20
may be selected. Never silently save or discard a prefix.

### Constructing and validating anchors

Candidate construction:

1. Compare frozen selected wording with the owner's Intended wording.
2. Use existing ordered lexical alignment to identify individual operations.
3. Construct each candidate independently: its intended phrase contains only
   that operation, not neighboring unselected changes.
4. Preserve exact punctuation and separators. Lexical token spans alone are not
   a complete character-edit script.

For each candidate, the unchanged prefix and suffix surrounding its individual
change must be identical on both sides. Replacing the before-change range with
the after-change wording must reproduce the complete reusable intended phrase
exactly.

For every submitted mapping, the server independently derives lexical changes
from the reusable phrase pair using the existing lexical vocabulary. Require
exactly one lexical change corresponding to the declared operation, valid
change spans locating that repair, exact reconstruction of the intended phrase,
and compliance with the applicable anchor rule. Reject pairs with no lexical
change, mislabeled operations, pairs containing additional lexical changes,
and a purported replacement that merely encloses an addition/removal in
unchanged wording. Submitted spans cannot bypass operation classification.

For an addition/removal, the owner can expand the before range within frozen
Original. Display the expanded phrase before approval. Construct the intended
phrase by applying only the selected individual operation to that expanded
source range. Expansion must not cross another changed range or silently
incorporate another repair.

An addition/removal pair must contain at least one complete unchanged adjacent
lexical token. This is a mechanical minimum, not a claim of sufficient context
in every passage. The owner reviews and explicitly selects the displayed anchor.
If alignment cannot isolate the operation without choosing among ambiguous
locations or incorporating another change, mark it unsaveable and explain that
its source range or Intended wording must be narrowed or adjusted. Do not search
for another occurrence, invent an anchor or relabel the operation. Replacement
candidates require nonempty changed wording on both sides, but no unchanged anchor.

Bounds:

- Each reusable phrase side: at most 200 code points.
- Shared restriction: at most 500 code points.
- Each optional sentence: at most 500 code points.
- Full transient source revisions: retain the existing 100,000-code-point request bound.

Convert browser UTF-16 selection offsets explicitly to and from code-point
offsets. Validate source slices before displaying or approving candidates.
HTML `maxlength` is not authoritative because it counts UTF-16 units. Client
validation must permit otherwise-valid text within the code-point bounds;
server validation remains authoritative. Retain field-specific private-text
and control-character validation. Never silently truncate durable fields.
Over-limit sentence previews must be edited or removed.

### Mapping identity and independent state

One entry represents one reusable mapping. Distinct before phrases do not share
an enable/edit/revision unit merely because their Intended wording matches.
Identity is local owner, operation, normalized reusable before, normalized
reusable intended, and exact approved restriction. Use existing NFKC,
apostrophe, casefold and whitespace normalization for phrase keys. Significant
punctuation remains significant.

Absent or blank-only restrictions become no restriction. Otherwise retain and
compare exact text; do not casefold, paraphrase or infer semantic equivalence.
Each mapping owns its enabled state and revision. Saving or managing another
mapping cannot alter either.

Equivalent saves reuse the mapping and create a new approval episode. Preserve
canonical mapping display text while recording the exact wording approved in
the new episode. Differing restrictions remain distinct. Saving an equivalent
disabled mapping does not re-enable it.

Keep the existing persistence subsystem and episode/event relationships:

- Extend entries with operation and reusable before/intended identity.
- Existing `intended_term` may remain the physical column for intended phrase
  text; domain/API names use `reusable_intended_text`.
- Retain alias rows needed for existing references, without a second independent
  authorization lifecycle. A new entry has one corresponding before-phrase alias.

The approved [#204 migration exception](https://github.com/dmclark/sayloom/issues/204)
replaces legacy splitting and evidence reassignment with refusal when any
correction content exists. It does not invent triggers or convert legacy records.

### Source-specific bindings and API

Extend existing candidate and episode endpoints with `personal-correction-v2`.

**Original selection:** bind review to frozen Original, passage identity, draft
revision, initial selection and expanded source ranges. Candidate derivation
accepts the transient Original snapshot and owner-supplied Intended wording.

**Manual Prepared edit:** retain the existing binding to frozen starting
Prepared and owner-confirmed wording, together with the existing Original
revision, Prepared identity/generation and confirmation-validity checks.
Candidate derivation accepts those transient before/after Prepared revisions.
It does not require or invent an Original selection.

Both variants have a review identity and converge on the same bounded
mapping/approval representation. Preserve their distinct source labels and
invalidation rules.

`POST /api/personal-corrections/candidates` accepts the source-specific binding
and transient text required by that variant. Return echoed review and
source-binding identifiers, candidates, bounded sentence previews, current
authorization generation and running application-instance identity.

Candidate derivation writes nothing. Full revisions remain request-scoped,
with no database retention, request-body logging or echoed private values in
errors. Manual review requires schema/application readiness, not Smart
availability or a processing prompt. Its route must not perform the current
unconditional active-prompt lookup.

`POST /api/personal-corrections/episodes` accepts:

- Approval UUID and review identity.
- Source discriminator and source-specific bounded binding metadata.
- Application-instance identity returned during candidate derivation.
- Manual provenance: `source_mode = user_confirmed`, no processing prompt.
- Expected authorization generation.
- Ordered selected mappings, including individual-change spans.
- Shared restriction and optional approved sentences.

Original-selection binding metadata includes passage/revision and
initial/expanded source offsets. Manual Prepared binding metadata includes the
relevant Prepared binding identities/revisions, with no invented
Original-selection offsets. Approval contains no full Original or Prepared
snapshots. The server validates bounded representation and transaction
preconditions. The browser establishes whether its live passage still matches
the frozen review. Client-supplied metadata is not independent server proof of
the live draft. Responses identify the approval and review concerned; they
cannot authorize reopening stale review.

A committed response contains immutable episode/mapping details and affected
mapping references. Return current mapping/profile state separately from the
immutable historical approval. Add owner-scoped approval-detail lookup and a
bounded, paginated recent-approval list in the existing management area. These
support reload recovery without storing unsaved review in browser storage.
A missing result is not proof that no save ever occurred. All content-bearing
responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`. Errors contain no submitted private wording.

### Atomicity, retries and conflicts

Store immutable approved fields in episode and episode-mapping records:
exact approved wording, restriction and change spans; source-specific binding
metadata; review identity; expected authorization generation; and
application-instance identity. Do not add a duplicate full JSON payload store.
Define canonical serialization of these fields for exact retry comparison.
Compare ordering and exact approved display text. Phrase normalization determines
mapping identity, not permission to alter an approval payload.

Serialize approval writers through SQLite's write-transaction boundary before
reading conflict-sensitive state:

1. Look up the owner-scoped approval UUID.
2. If committed, compare its immutable payload and return that outcome or conflict.
3. For a new approval, validate application-instance identity and authorization generation.
4. Validate all mappings and resolve equivalent identities.
5. Create missing mappings, episode, ordered approval rows and capture events.
6. Commit everything together.

Database uniqueness constraints provide a backstop:

- Same ID and payload: return the committed receipt.
- Same ID with changed payload: HTTP 409, no writes.
- Invalid representation or operation: HTTP 422, no writes.
- Stale instance or conflicting authorization generation: HTTP 409, no writes.
- Transaction failure: roll back the entire batch.
- Lost transport response: outcome unknown until retry or reconciliation.

Resolve an already committed retry before checking the current instance or
generation. Its original instance identity remains part of its immutable
payload. After restart, an identical request can reconcile a commit; changing
its instance identity under the same approval UUID conflicts.

An absent profile is generation zero. First approved save creates an enabled
profile at generation zero only when no profile exists. Preserve existing global
disablement. Every profile toggle or management mutation advances generation
transactionally and uses an expected-generation/revision check. Ordinary new
approvals do not advance it, so unrelated saves do not invalidate one another.
This conservative generation may reject review after an unrelated management
mutation, but cannot overwrite newer state. If first saves race, the serialized
second transaction sees the first profile and preserves its state. No save
resets disablement. Explicit profile enablement cannot create an absent profile;
first approved save remains the creation boundary.

Collapse exact repeated mappings visibly before approval. If
normalized-equivalent candidates have incompatible display/change
representations, require the owner to choose one. Do not choose silently inside
the save transaction.

### Staleness and uncertain saves

An unsent Original review becomes stale after Original editing, Undo/Redo,
Clear or passage replacement; replacement with identical text under a new
passage/revision identity; another review opening; reload or application restart;
or a conflicting management change. Settings alone do not invalidate manual
Intended wording. Prepared review retains its existing source-specific validity
checks.

Late candidate responses cannot reopen dismissed or superseded review. A new
application-instance identity invalidates old unsaved review rather than silently
rebinding it. Once Save is sent:

- Freeze the selected payload and approval UUID.
- Do not permit altered-payload retry under that UUID.
- Subsequent editing retires browser effects but cannot cancel a server commit.
- Late success cannot modify Original, Prepared or Outgoing.
- While review remains current, explicit retry sends the identical approval.
- After reload or staleness, use management reconciliation rather than
  reconstructing an altered approval.

No save failure starts processing or a provider retry.

### Prepared compatibility and legacy writes

Keep existing Prepared confirmation and its separate source-validity checks.
Do not loosen them globally to accommodate Original review. Update both browser
saving paths to v2. Preserve source labels, unchecked selections and the rule
that confirming Prepared saves nothing.

Retain read access to legacy records, but not a second v1 saving implementation:

- v1 candidate/approval submissions receive a structured version-conflict
  response instructing reload; rejected requests write nothing.
- Existing approvals remain inspectable using their recorded version.
- Do not invent missing historical fields or promise retroactive v2 retry behavior.

Retire direct `POST /entries` creation and remove the direct-add form. Disable
the legacy destructive entry-update path for migrated mappings, including
mappings reused by v2 approval. Protection depends on the mapping and its
evidence links, not merely an episode version. Until reviewed management is
separately delivered, mapping fields are inspectable but not editable through
that legacy path. Global disable/re-enable remains revision-checked;
independent per-mapping management is later work.

### Events and privacy lifecycle

Retain `alias_observed` for explicit capture. It records the before phrase
represented in an explicitly approved capture, at most once per approval/mapping.
It is not a processing encounter, correctness judgment or verified speech.
Do not create `profile_supplied` or `intended_produced` during manual saving.
Capture events commit with approval. No additional feedback or activity-history
capture is introduced.

Only approved bounded wording is durable. Retain no full Original/Prepared
snapshots or raw provider output, credentials in correction records, or private
wording in errors, diagnostics or request logs. Development-state databases
remain independent. Historical sentences never become future processing evidence.

Future hard deletion must cover private approval fields and shared-episode
consequences. Do not retain hidden receipt copies outside that scope. Deletion
and post-deletion idempotency behavior belong to later management design; this
increment promises neither deletion nor indefinite receipt retention.

`0006_confirmed_correction_batches`. Under the approved #204 exception, check

or data. Any populated guarded table causes a content-free refusal requiring

For an eligible empty store, introduce operation and complete mapping-identity

Normal startup reports schema readiness only; it never migrates or seeds.

### Acceptance and remaining gates

Later implementation must verify:

- **Independent entry:** preparation Off, no Prepared/finding and unavailable
  provider; zero provider calls.
- **Source variants:** Original selection and manual Prepared review retain
  their own truthful bindings and validity rules.
- **Correct binding:** repeated wording, identical-text passage replacement,
  stale responses and supplementary Unicode.
- **Operations:** server classification rejects mislabeled, no-change and
  multi-change pairs; valid anchored pairs reconstruct exactly.
- **Mixed passages:** selected mappings exclude neighboring unselected repairs.
- **Identity/state:** equivalent phrases reuse one mapping; restrictions
  coexist; siblings have independent state; disabled equivalents remain disabled.
- **Limits:** both sides of code-point limits, including supplementary
  characters; no truncation.
- **Transactions:** concurrent first saves, equivalent saves and management
  races; injected failure leaves no partial records.
- **Receipts:** lost-response retry creates one episode; changed payload
  conflicts; restart allows committed reconciliation but rejects new
  stale-instance approval.
- **Profile creation:** first save creates an enabled profile; an existing
  disabled profile remains disabled.
- **No implicit effects:** opening, editing, dismissing, confirming Prepared,
  copying and delivery save nothing; saving changes no output.
- **Compatibility:** Prepared review works through v2; retired endpoints
  cannot bypass approval or destroy evidence.
- **Migration:** absent and content-free disabled/enabled profiles upgrade without
  seeds or unrelated configuration changes; each populated guarded table causes
  refusal with schema revision and all data unchanged. Incompatible downgrade

## Complete-loop design and evidence boundary

The owner confirmed the retained Smart-loop design in [the complete-loop design session](https://ampcode.com/threads/T-01a12c59-2533-768a-810a-3458f41436b1).
The [closed design decision's resolution](https://github.com/dmclark/sayloom/issues/163#issuecomment-6113213278)
is provenance for that scope, not approval of flag-only assistance or runtime
acceptance. PRODUCT.md explicitly extends contextual mapping use to inspection-first
assistance; the approved bounded contract above defines the selected adaptation.
The following Smart-loop requirements retain their broader scope without
making automatic Smart completion a prerequisite for inspection-first delivery.

The contract is **repair → review → explicitly save → contextual reuse**:

- Smart puts inferred wording directly into Prepared. Successful Smart repairs
  can open a separate save review without retyping. Neither **Looks right** nor
  copying/delivery saves a correction.
- Successful Smart repairs, user corrections to Prepared, and valid bound
  Original-edit capture share one explicit save review. It identifies the
  before/after source, starts selections unchecked, previews all durable fields,
  and atomically saves 1–20 selected mappings. Generated changes outside a
  Prepared edit are not proposals from that edit.
- Additions, removals, and replacements all support reuse. An addition/removal
  needs an explicitly approved short before → intended phrase pair containing
  enough unchanged wording to anchor it. Review shows the individual operation
  and editable reusable pair. Without a meaningful anchor, the edit may remain
  in Prepared but is not yet saveable for reuse. Empty triggers are not rules.
- Exact normalized trigger matching retrieves candidates. Smart uses the
  current draft, selected Writing domain, and approved applicability restriction
  to apply or abstain. Literal, contradictory, inappropriate, and unresolved
  ambiguous uses remain unchanged. Correctly recognized intended wording stays
  unchanged. Matching does not authorize unconditional replacement.
- Differing intended phrases or applicability restrictions remain separate;
  equivalent mappings may be deduplicated. No save overwrites another mapping
  or silently broadens its restriction. Recency/counts do not resolve ambiguity.
- One management area covers all operations. Beginning an edit suspends reuse;
  explicit reviewed save authorizes the revision. Cancelling retains the prior
  wording disabled. Explicit re-enable of an unchanged correction restores its
  existing authorization. Management never silently rewrites current Prepared.
- Optional historical sentences are bounded, previewed, editable/removable,
  and never supplied to Smart. Events distinguish observed, supplied, and
  produced for every reusable operation without correctness/causation claims.
- Download private export is a management action with no retained server copy.
  Live-database deletion scopes and shared-episode cascades remain binding.
  Managed-backup policy stays deferred and blocks complete durable-deletion
  acceptance; user-created exports are not removed by deletion.

Every lexical change remains reviewable with advisory uncertainty when Original
is visible. Review does not gate copying/delivery; Outgoing is the exact visible
selected value. CART has no inline correction markings. Core Cleanup remains
independent of Personal Corrections.

The [merged Prepared-confirmation delivery](https://github.com/dmclark/sayloom/pull/167)
records exact manual confirmation and optional atomic selected-replacement
saving only. It does not complete [ordered mixed mappings](https://github.com/dmclark/sayloom/issues/150)
or this loop. Correct Prepared requires current Prepared; Original-seeded

The phrase “red cat delivery” → “Raycast delivery” is discussion evidence for

No additional private recognition-repair record was inspected or reconstructed.

## Confirmed outcome and non-goals

The first release serves one implicit private local principal with durable
`owner_id = "local"`. It adds no account, login, owner selector, or owner UI.
The user can:

1. turn a successful Smart repair or a user-supplied repair into a reviewed
   correction episode;
2. explicitly review and approve an Original → Intended mapping;
3. save a Personal Correction for live use in later sessions;
4. inspect, edit, disable, or delete a Personal correction;
5. have matching enabled replacement or anchored addition/removal mappings
   contextually inform later inspection-first findings and optional suggestions
   as well as Smart correction, without affecting Cleanup;
6. inspect content-minimized recurrence events; and
7. export the current private state in a versioned machine-readable format.

The release does not add hosted identity, multiple users, device sync, model
training, cross-user learning, automatic approval, outcome claims, `helped` or
`wrong` feedback, formulaic Cleanup rules, or evaluation evidence. Accounts
remain deferred until multi-user, device-sync, or hosting boundaries exist.
Any renewed research requires a new approved evaluation purpose and contract.

Core Cleanup remains account-independent. No Personal correction setting,
entry, alias, episode, event, lookup, failure, or export may change Cleanup's
eligibility, prompt, request, result validation, or fallback behavior.

## Current implementation seams

The implementation must extend, rather than bypass, these current boundaries:

- `ApplicationDatabase` and Alembic own the SQLite schema. Startup compares the
  database revision with `SCHEMA_REVISION`; it does not migrate or seed.
- Stores use short SQLAlchemy transactions and the database enables SQLite
  foreign keys on every connection.
- `/api/cleanup` resolves the active prompt and delegates to `CleanupService`.
  `CleanupService` already accepts request-scoped Smart context and curated
  knowledge, while Conservative requests reject either form of evidence.
- Smart evidence is framed as untrusted JSON for the provider. Personal
  corrections must become typed server-selected evidence within that frame;
  historical sentences must not enter it.
- The browser's `draftRevision`, request sequence, abort controller, exact
  Original, mode, and task-context comparisons reject stale processing results.
  Missed-repair state must use the same invalidation boundary.
- Existing lexical review uses ordered additions, removals, and replacements
  with validated spans. Candidate derivation should share that vocabulary and
  implementation rather than introduce a second diff contract.

Ordinary `/api/cleanup` callers must not submit profile rows or select an owner.
The application route owns local-principal selection and retrieval before it
constructs the internal Smart request. Evidence Collection is retired and is
not a Personal Corrections write path.

## Terms used here

- **Local principal:** The constant internal owner key `local`. It is not a
  hosted account or display name.
- **Profile:** The local principal's persisted Personal Corrections enablement
  state and entries.
- **Entry:** A managed reusable correction with approved before → intended
  wording, operation, applicability restriction, authorization/enabled state,
  and linked history. Replacement-oriented storage below is a starting point,
  not a complete representation of the approved model.
- **Episode:** One explicit approval containing selected before/after mappings
  and optional bounded observed and intended sentences.
- **Use ID:** A random UUID identifying one capture approval or one Smart
  request for idempotent event recording. It contains no text.

## Durable model requirements and replacement-oriented schema reference

For the first increment, the [approved technical design](#approved-provider-free-original-reviewsave-technical-design)
supersedes the retained replacement-oriented shapes below, including grouped
alias authorization, intended-term-only uniqueness and v1-only approval fields.
These references do not replace its one-entry-per-mapping lifecycle.

All content lives in the existing private application SQLite database. IDs are
random UUID strings. Timestamps are UTC. Every content-bearing table carries
`owner_id`, and every store query includes `owner_id = "local"`; route-only
filtering is insufficient.

**Schema reference, not a complete-loop implementation prescription:** the
tables below preserve the earlier replacement-oriented design. In particular,
canonical-term-only uniqueness, replacement-only links, and generated-result
provenance need revision. Complete-loop approval requires reusable anchored
additions/removals, separately scoped mappings, and user-confirmed provenance.
Do not force empty operations into aliases or treat these older shapes as a
reason to narrow the confirmed product design.

## Validation and normalization

Length limits are measured in Unicode code points in both request validation
and SQLite `CHECK` constraints. Text must be valid UTF-8 after JSON decoding,
must not contain NUL or other disallowed control characters, and is stored in
the exact user-approved display form.

The initial normalization key is deterministic and locale-independent:

1. Unicode NFKC normalization;
2. replace curly apostrophe `’` with ASCII apostrophe `'`;
3. Unicode `casefold`;
4. collapse every run of Unicode whitespace to one ASCII space; and
5. trim leading and trailing whitespace.

Stage 1 performs no stemming, phonetic matching, punctuation deletion, fuzzy
matching, or synonym expansion. Any broadening requires evidence from observed
misses and a later explicit decision.

## Bounded observed and intended sentences

When editing begins after a current Cleanup or Smart result, the browser freezes
the exact pre-edit Original in memory. During the same editing session, each
input refreshes the prospective post-edit Original and restarts the existing
cleanup debounce. The latest revision becomes the exact post-edit Original when
the edit settles or the user invokes **This is what I meant**. The browser
derives the changed range from the two bound revisions' common prefix and
suffix, then prefills the sentence containing that range on each side. Sentence
boundaries are the nearest preceding and following `.`, `?`, `!`, or line
break; the start or end of the value is the fallback. Delimiters remain in the
preview.

The preview never includes a neighboring sentence. A sentence longer than 500
code points may be shown ephemerally, but approval remains disabled until the
user edits it to at most 500 code points or removes it. The API and database
independently reject an over-limit value. Truncation is never silent. Both
observed and intended values are visible and independently editable/removable
before save.

This deliberately simple segmentation is an implementation boundary, not a
linguistic claim. Abbreviations and punctuation-free dictation may produce a
larger preview, but the storage limit remains absolute.

## Capture and approval

The [approved first-increment design](#approved-provider-free-original-reviewsave-technical-design)
owns Original-selected review and manual Prepared bindings. The retained
generated-result Original-edit state machine and invalidation list below do
not impose result/prompt prerequisites on manual review, invalidate manual
Intended wording on settings changes, or roll back an already committed save.

### Shared review entry paths

Successful Smart repair review uses that repair's bound before/after values.
Correct Prepared uses only its frozen starting Prepared and confirmed wording.
The Original-edit path below retains its exact pre/post binding. Review names
the source rather than implying every before value is the Original field.

All paths show unchecked candidates, individual changes, approved reusable
phrase pairs, applicability restrictions, and optional bounded sentences. Save
is separate from judging a repair correct, confirming Prepared, or delivery.
Dismissal or save failure leaves confirmed Prepared and Outgoing intact.

For manual Prepared review, Original changes, another correction opening, or
Regenerate make it stale; settings changes do not. No provider or invented
prompt provenance is needed. The state machine and input-change rules below
describe the generated-result Original-edit path, not a rule that settings
changes may invalidate a manual confirmation.

### Original-edit capture state machine

```text
┌──────┐  current result  ┌──────────┐  first Original edit  ┌───────────┐
│ Idle │─────────────────▶│ Eligible │───────────────────────▶│ Available │
└──────┘                  └──────────┘                        └─────┬─────┘
                                                                  │ explicit
                                                                  │ action
                                                                  ▼
                                                            ┌───────────┐
                                                            │ Reviewing │
                                                            └─────┬─────┘
                                                                  │ save
                                                                  ▼
                                                            ┌──────────┐
                                                            │ Saving  │
                                                            └──┬────┬──┘
                                                       retryable │    │ success
                                                          failure│    ▼
                                                                 │ ┌─────┐
                                                                 └▶│Saved│
                                                                   └─────┘

Any invalidating action: Eligible / Available / Reviewing / Saving → Stale → Idle
Dismissal: Available / Reviewing → Idle, with no write
```

### Binding a missed result

`Eligible` exists only for a current Cleanup or Smart result and snapshots:

- result/request sequence;
- draft revision and exact pre-edit Original;
- mode, task context, prompt version, and correction contract version; and
- Prepared-result identity needed to prove that the result was current.

The first Original input starts one editing session. Further input before review
updates its post-edit revision rather than making the interaction stale; this is
required for ordinary multi-keystroke and dictation edits. `Available` begins
when that editing session settles for the cleanup debounce interval, or when the
user invokes the action, and binds the latest exact post-edit revision. The
automatic regeneration for that same revision may complete without invalidating
`Available`; otherwise the interaction could disappear before use. That
response must match the exact post-edit revision and transformation inputs.
After review opens, any further edit is a new revision and makes the review
stale. Any unrelated result replacement is also stale.

### Review and save

**This is what I meant** derives ordered addition, removal, and replacement
candidates from the frozen pre/post revisions. It does not alter Original or
Prepared. Review shows every candidate unselected by default, both bounded
sentences, and all durable text. The user may select 1–20 mappings, edit the
mapping text, edit or remove either sentence, and edit/remove applicability
context.

The interface describes this action as **save a Personal Correction**, never as
“data collection.” Save requires explicit review and approval of the displayed
Original → Intended mapping. That approval authorizes the saved correction for
live Smart use across later sessions until it is edited, disabled, or deleted.
Beginning a management edit suspends the prior mapping's reuse; the edited
mapping must be reviewed and explicitly saved before reuse. Cancelling retains
the previous wording disabled. Explicit re-enable of unchanged wording restores
its existing authorization.

Save is one database transaction. For each selected reusable mapping it:

1. validates the operation and its reviewed reusable before/intended pair;
2. normalizes the pair and deduplicates only an equivalent owned mapping with
   the same applicability restriction, or creates an enabled mapping;
3. preserves differing intended wording or restrictions as separate mappings;
4. creates the episode and ordered mapping rows; and
5. creates idempotent capture `alias_observed` events.

The unique `approval_id` makes a retry after a lost response return the original
saved episode rather than duplicate it. An equivalent mapping adds recurrence
to the equivalent mapping; it does not create a duplicate. Saving a differing
restriction does not replace an existing one. A deliberate management edit
requires explicit review/save and cannot silently broaden another mapping.

Ordinary editing, regeneration, Copy, target delivery, Outgoing selection,
inaction, dismissal, closing the dialog, or leaving the page writes nothing.

### Invalidation

The following make a bound review or in-flight save stale immediately:

- another Original edit after review binds the post-edit revision;
- Clear, a new draft, or replace-all input;
- mode or task-context change;
- prompt/version change;
- manual regeneration not bound to the exact post-edit revision;
- a stale or out-of-order response;
- result replacement for different inputs;
- page reload or application restart; or
- deletion or revision change of an entry being edited in management.

A stale Save is rejected before a request is sent. Server responses carry the
saved entry revisions; management writes use `expected_revision` and return
HTTP 409 on conflict. A 409 never overwrites or deletes newer state.

## API boundary

The [approved first-increment API design](#source-specific-bindings-and-api)
owns v2 manual candidate/approval requests, source-specific bindings and

route reference below does not add management, deletion, export or retrieval
implementation to that increment.

The following replacement-oriented route reference requires payload revision
for anchored operations and all three review sources; it is not a new API
implementation authorization. All routes select the constant
local principal internally, call `require_configuration`, use privacy-safe
validation errors, and return `Cache-Control: no-store` for content-bearing
responses.

| Method and route | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/personal-corrections` | Return global state, entries, aliases, bounded episodes, and content-minimized history for management. |
| `PUT /api/personal-corrections/profile` | Globally disable or re-enable an existing local profile. Accept only `enabled`; it cannot create an absent profile. |
| `PUT /api/personal-corrections/entries/{id}` | Revision-checked save of a reviewed edit to intended term, aliases, context, or enabled state; changed mapping text is not reusable before this explicit save. |

| `POST /api/personal-corrections/candidates` | Derive ordered lexical mappings and sentence prefills from exact pre/post revisions without writing them. |

The candidate request accepts the same 100,000-code-point maximum per full

episode request contains `approval_id`, bound mode/version metadata, only the

Ordinary `/api/cleanup` remains the only processing route. Its public input
keeps Original, mode, and optional Smart task context but does not accept
Personal corrections payloads or owner IDs. The route performs server-owned
retrieval and constructs the internal request described below.

## Deterministic Smart retrieval

**Retained Smart-specific reference:** The following algorithm does not specify
inspection-first detection or mapping influence. Exact normalized candidate
retrieval and applicability controls remain required, but a match alone justifies
neither a replacement nor a mapping-derived warning. No new retrieval algorithm,
endpoint, field or event shape is selected here.

Retrieval runs only when all of these are true:

- mode is Smart;
- the local profile row exists and is globally enabled;
- the entry and alias are enabled by virtue of the entry being enabled; and
- the normalized approved trigger occurs in normalized Original at a lexical
  boundary. For an addition/removal this is the anchored before phrase, not an
  empty alias or a word to delete everywhere.

A lexical boundary means an alias beginning or ending with a Unicode letter,
number, mark, or underscore cannot be adjacent to the same class in Original.
Punctuation otherwise remains significant. Matching retains normalized offsets
only in memory for result comparison; offsets are not persisted.

Matches sort by:

1. earliest normalized start offset;
2. longest normalized alias first;
3. `alias_key`;
4. `intended_key`; and
5. entry UUID.

Duplicate occurrences of the same alias-entry pair in one Original produce one
retrieval item and one event of each applicable type. Up to 50 unique mappings
may be supplied, matching the current bounded Smart-knowledge request limit. A
51st match causes profile retrieval to fail open as a whole: Smart continues
without Personal corrections, no occurrence rows are written for that request,
and the UI exposes a content-free safe diagnostic. It must not send a silently
partial or order-dependent profile.

Each retrieved item keeps entry and alias IDs only in local request state for
event correlation. The typed object serialized inside the existing untrusted
Smart-evidence JSON frame contains only the approved reusable before/intended
pair, its operation, and applicability restriction. Observed/intended sentences,
local IDs, other aliases, disabled entries, recurrence counts, and events are never sent to the
provider. The Smart prompt treats mappings as user-owned evidence, using the
current draft and selected Writing domain to judge applicability. It must
abstain for literal, contradictory, inappropriate, or unresolved ambiguous
uses. Differing meanings/restrictions are supplied as separate candidates;
recency and counts cannot decide between them. Correctly recognized intended
wording stays unchanged. Retrieval order is not a replacement priority.

The public cleanup request model must no longer accept ordinary caller-supplied
profile content. The route builds a separate internal Smart request after
server-side retrieval. Conservative mode reaches `CleanupService` with no task
context or profile evidence exactly as it does now.

## Failure behavior

Personal corrections must never remove access to Original, block Copy or
delivery, or convert a Cleanup request into Smart.

| Failure | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Profile read, normalization, or retrieval fails | Run Smart without profile evidence; show a content-free retryable diagnostic. |
| Occurrence write fails before Smart handoff | Do not supply profile evidence; run Smart without it so `profile_supplied` is not falsely claimed. |
| Provider, timeout, or result validation fails | Preserve existing fail-open-to-Original behavior; retain only successfully committed observed/supplied events. |
| `intended_produced` write fails after a valid result | Keep the Prepared result and review queue; show history as incomplete without logging text. |
| Candidate derivation fails | Preserve current Original, confirmed Prepared, and Outgoing eligibility; save remains unavailable. |
| Approval validation fails | Keep current review state in browser memory and identify fields to fix; write nothing. |
| Approval transaction fails | Roll back every entry, alias, episode, mapping, and event write; allow idempotent retry while current. |
| Management/export read fails | Leave durable state unchanged and return a content-free safe error. |
| Export is interrupted | Leave no server-side artifact; a partial browser download is not a successful export. |

Validation handlers for Personal corrections routes must not echo request
bodies or private text. Routine logs may contain operation, safe error code,
row counts, durations, and content lengths, but not terms, aliases, context,
sentences, profile JSON, exports, or provider payloads.

## Management and deletion

Personal corrections is one user-visible management area for all repair types.
It shows global enablement, approved before/intended pairs, individual
operations, applicability restrictions, per-correction enabled state,
content-minimized event counts/history, and linked bounded episode evidence.
Exact layout is an implementation choice; hiding any durable field is not.

An entry update is transactional, owner-scoped, and revision-checked. Disabling
an entry prevents future retrieval but preserves its episodes and history.
Global disablement prevents all retrieval but preserves entries.

Beginning an edit suspends that correction's reuse. Explicit review/save
authorizes revised wording; cancellation leaves the previous wording retained
but disabled. Explicit re-enable of unchanged wording restores its existing
authorization. Neither management nor save silently regenerates or rewrites
current Prepared. Conflict handling remains revision-checked and never
overwrites newer state.

UI deletion is scoped to the currently active development state. It must not
claim to remove the correction from another state. A separate administration
command may purge Personal Corrections across registered states only. Before
writing, that command must preview the affected registered states and require
explicit confirmation; unregistered directories are outside its scope.

Entry deletion is a hard delete. In one transaction it deletes:

- the entry and every alias;
- every linked occurrence event;
- every episode containing a mapping linked to that entry, including that
  episode's other mapping rows and sentence fields; and
- no unrelated entry.

Deleting the whole shared episode is intentionally privacy-favoring: sentence
evidence that supported a deleted entry must not remain indirectly reachable
through another mapping. Surviving profile entries remain, but recurrence from
that shared episode disappears. The confirmation states this consequence.
Deleted content is absent from all later API responses and exports; there are
no tombstones or application audit copies.

Removing an individual alias uses the same privacy rule at alias scope: it
deletes that alias, its events, and every episode containing a mapping linked to
it, while preserving the entry and its other aliases. Editing alias text is a
revision-checked entry update; episode mapping text remains the exact approved
historical value, while future retrieval uses the edited alias. The management
confirmation distinguishes edit from deletion and states the deletion cascade.

Deletion claims must name their scope: current-state UI deletion or confirmed
purge across the previewed registered states. Neither flow claims deletion from
unregistered directories, managed SQLite backups, or user-created exports.

**Deferred privacy choice — blocks complete durable-deletion acceptance:** The
controlled restart workflow creates full SQLite backups. D15 settles deletion
from live current-state and registered-state databases only; it does not decide
how backups containing Personal Corrections are handled. Before implementation
claims complete durable deletion, confirm one of these policies:

1. automatic lifecycle backups omit Personal Corrections tables;
2. deletion also removes every in-scope managed backup that may contain
   Personal Corrections, with a clear warning about loss of configuration
   recovery; or
3. deletion covers the selected live databases and future exports while prior
   encrypted or access-controlled recovery snapshots follow a separately
   documented retention period.

The versioned private export remains user-created private data under every
option and is never silently removed by UI deletion or registered-state purge.

## Versioned private export

`GET /api/personal-corrections/export` produces UTF-8 JSON with
`Content-Disposition: attachment`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, and media type
`application/vnd.local-text.personal-corrections+json;version=1`. The server
does not retain an export file.

The top-level object is:

```json
{
  "schema_version": "personal-corrections-export-v1",
  "exported_at": "<UTC RFC 3339 timestamp>",
  "profile": {
    "enabled": true,
    "entries": [],
    "episodes": [],
    "events": []
  }
}
```

Entries contain ID, intended term, approved context, enabled state, revision,
timestamps, and aliases with IDs and timestamps. Episodes contain ID, approval
ID, optional sentences, source mode, prompt version ID, contract version,
timestamp, and ordered mappings. Events contain ID, linked IDs, use ID, event
type, and timestamp. Normalization keys and `owner_id` are internal and omitted.

Arrays sort by creation time then ID; mappings sort by ordinal. The export is a
snapshot from one read transaction. Empty or never-enabled state still exports
the version, timestamp, `enabled: false`, and empty arrays. Deleted rows and
unrelated application configuration are absent. Import is not part of stage 1.

**Confirmed delivery surface:** Personal Corrections management offers
**Download private export**. It emits one versioned snapshot and writes no
second server-managed copy. The replacement-oriented v1 representation above
needs technical reconciliation for anchored operations and distinct
restrictions before implementation. Import remains deferred.

## Development-state location and inheritance

The development-state root moves from the current macOS Application Support
location to `${XDG_DATA_HOME:-~/.local/share}/local-text/dev-states/`. A
dedicated administration operation performs and verifies that migration before
activation. Normal startup never moves state files or runs database migrations.

After Personal Corrections are implemented, creation of a new development
state must inherit them when an eligible source was explicitly activated for
that creation. The source is read in one transaction, copied as a coherent
snapshot, and verified in the destination before activation. API keys and other
protected shared configuration are not part of the Personal Corrections
snapshot.

If an eligible source exists and snapshot creation, copy, or verification
fails, initialization fails before activation and preserves the source. It must
not silently substitute an empty profile. If no eligible source exists,
initialization reports an explicitly empty Personal Corrections profile and may
activate the state. Creation performs no backfill into existing states and no
post-creation synchronization; parallel states may diverge.

## Privacy invariants

1. One implicit local principal owns every Personal corrections query and row.
   No API accepts an owner selector.
2. Only explicit approval writes knowledge. Editing, Copy, delivery, Outgoing,
   inaction, dismissal, and model-repair review do not.
3. Full Original, Prepared, Outgoing, task context, and surrounding documents
   never enter Personal corrections tables, exports, errors, or routine logs.
4. Both optional sentence fields are previewed, editable/removable, bounded to
   500 code points, and enforced at API and storage boundaries.
5. Historical sentences and occurrence history never enter processing requests,
   including inspection-first requests; no new historical-context channel is created.
6. Only matching enabled approved mappings from an enabled profile may inform
   inspection-first assistance or Smart. Personal Corrections never inform Cleanup.
7. Profile evidence is advice, not a replacement command or automatic warning.
   Contextual findings may have no alternative; lexical Smart changes remain
   under the Smart review contract. Ordinary inspection or application saves nothing.
8. Disablement is reversible retention. Deletion is hard removal within its
   declared current-state or confirmed registered-state live-database scope.
   Managed-backup handling is unresolved, so complete durable deletion must not
   be claimed.
9. Export contains only Personal corrections state and writes no server-side
   copy. It is private data, not a support artifact or Git attachment.
10. Reactive episodes are excluded from held-out evaluation unless a future
    approved evaluation purpose and contract independently qualifies them.
11. API keys remain in protected shared configuration and never enter Personal
    Corrections tables, snapshots, or exports. Keychain storage is deferred.
12. Each development state owns an independent snapshot. New-state inheritance
    is transactional and verified, never silently empty when a source exists,
    and never followed by automatic synchronization.

### Persistence and migration

- A fresh migration creates an empty, disabled-by-absence profile with no seed
  write; predecessor configuration survives upgrade and rollback tests.
- Database checks reject over-limit/private-invalid text even when API
  validation is bypassed.
- Store tests prove every read/write/delete includes the local owner and that a
  foreign-owner fixture cannot be returned or mutated.
- Equivalent normalized replacements reuse one entry and alias while creating
  a distinct episode/event; differing context is never overwritten silently.
- Revision conflicts and repeated `approval_id` requests are deterministic.

### Capture and stale state

- Cover all three review entry paths, unchanged unchecked selections, atomic
  selected save, source identification, and no saving from repair confirmation.
- Prove anchored addition/removal reuse rather than episode-only storage, and
  preserve differing applicability restrictions without silent merging.
- Browser/API tests cover asymmetric addition, removal, and replacement
  examples whose expected mappings are not derived from production diff code.
- One Cleanup miss and one Smart miss reach review with exact pre/post binding
  and bounded editable sentences.
- A multi-keystroke edit retains one frozen pre-edit revision and binds the
  latest settled post-edit revision. Same-revision automatic regeneration
  preserves availability; every listed invalidating action makes review/save
  stale.
- Dismissal, ordinary edit without the explicit action, Copy, delivery,
  Outgoing selection, model-repair review, and inaction produce no write.
- A lost save response followed by retry creates one episode only; a failed
  transaction creates no partial row.

### Retrieval and correction isolation

- Include positive anchored addition/removal reuse and literal, contradictory,
  conflicting-mapping, inappropriate-context, and already-correct controls.
  Exact matching only retrieves candidates; a matched trigger must not force
  a repair. Smart writes repairs into Prepared, not suggestions beside it.
- Case, Unicode, apostrophe, whitespace, punctuation, lexical-boundary,
  duplicate-occurrence, ambiguous-alias, disabled-entry, and globally disabled
  cases prove the exact normalization and ordering contract.
- Fifty matches are deterministic; 51 fail open without partial evidence or
  occurrence rows.
- Smart provider payload inspection contains only matched mapping fields and
  approved context. It excludes sentences, other aliases, events, counts, and
  unrelated entries.
- Conservative/Cleanup requests and outputs are byte-for-byte unchanged by
  profile state, including when retrieval fails.
- Provider failure, timeout, malformed output, and event-write failure preserve
  the existing usable Original path.

### Events, deletion, and export

- Events distinguish observed, supplied, and intended-produced. An unchanged
  result, unrelated canonical text elsewhere, or invalid result does not create
  `intended_produced`.
- Deleting an entry removes aliases, linked events, and every linked episode
  while retaining unrelated entries; future export cannot reveal deleted text.
- Current-state deletion and preview/confirm cross-state purge scopes are
  exercised separately; neither claims to purge unregistered locations,
  managed backups, or user-created exports.
- The confirmed managed-backup policy is exercised, not merely documented,
  before complete durable deletion is accepted.
- Export validates against a checked-in v1 schema/fixture, has stable ordering,
  includes all approved fields and only content-minimized events, and emits no
  server-side artifact.

### Representative UI

- Desktop and narrow-browser checks cover disabled/empty, capture available,
  review with over-limit sentence, save failure/retry, ambiguous alias warning,
  disabled entry, delete confirmation, history, and export.
- DOM/accessibility checks prove Original remains editable, Prepared remains
  read-only, and capture or Personal corrections failures do not gate Copy,
  Outgoing, or target delivery.
