How it improves

Approved knowledge helps with recurring text errors.

Sayloom does not retrain Voiceitt or change its speech recognition. It uses what you explicitly approve to make better post-recognition suggestions for you.

After one sentence

Three useful questions

No survey, scoring exercise, or technical setup. The conversation is the feedback.

  1. Did the suggestion preserve what you meant?
  2. Was it clear what Sayloom changed and could remember?
  3. Would approved corrections reduce repeat effort in your normal writing?
Evidence before invitation

See the boundary clearly

Voiceitt listens.
Sayloom helps with the text.

Sayloom is an independent, unaffiliated companion. It never receives the recording and cannot know for certain what was spoken.

What it is

A correction and preparation layer

It sits after recognition and before the place where you want to use the text.

What it is not

Not a replacement for Voiceitt

It does not listen, transcribe speech, or claim a relationship with Voiceitt.

Five-minute demonstration

Explain, try, reflect

  1. Show one neutral prepared example and point out the marked word repair.
  2. Invite one ordinary sentence through the person’s normal Voiceitt workflow.
  3. Ask whether the result kept their meaning, showed its changes, and reduced effort.
